The Path of Humility

30 steps to overcome pride and grow from within

Pride rarely shouts. It hides in the need to be right, in comparison, in the difficulty of listening.

The Path of Humility is a practical 30-day program that brings together psychology and Christian spirituality to help you recognize pride, learn to listen, serve without expecting a return, and cultivate gratitude.

  • Kindle eBookAmazon and Kindle UnlimitedBuy Kindle eBook

How each day works

Morning

A short reflection from the book to begin the day with today’s step.

During the day

A practical exercise to live that step in ordinary situations.

Evening

A moment of reflection and prayer to look back on the day.

For groups and churches

The 30 days work well in small groups: each person takes the day’s step at home, and the group meets once a week to talk about the week. The app is free for everyone.

A free app to walk the 30 days

The app goes with The Path of Humility: each day’s exercise, written or voice notes, the Pride Thermometer, your week-by-week progress and your rule of life. It works on phones and computers, with no sign-up, and your data stays on your device.

  • Opens straight on Day 1, no sign-up
  • Can be installed on your home screen
  • In English, Portuguese and Spanish
