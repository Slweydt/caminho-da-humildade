About the author

A Christian, he holds a bachelor's degree in aeronautical sciences. He served as a fighter pilot in the Brazilian Air Force and later became an airline pilot, flying both cargo and passenger aircraft. Throughout his aviation career, he flew single-, twin-, three-, and four-engine jet aircraft. He also took part in public life as an alternate candidate for Brazil's Chamber of Deputies.
